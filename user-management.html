Accounts

Faculty-first setup: choose a faculty record and the profile supplies the name and email. Firebase creates the Authentication UID automatically. Office roles use their own display name and email, without linking a faculty record. Existing administrative accounts keep their current UID.

Access roles: Developer is the highest system role with full access to every administrative, scheduling, approval and DOE capability. Only Developer can create or modify Developer accounts. Owner controls User Management for roles below Developer. Administrator manages faculty and timetable workflows. Other Office sees only its own Change History. ADC manages scheduling fields; LAB manages LAB topics. Neither role has Faculty Dashboard access. HICC, VISC and Faculty are faculty-facing roles.

Faculty groups

HICC Groups

Each group belongs to an HICC. Developer / Owner assigns the HICC owner and course scope. HICCs can add or remove members in their own groups. Timetable changes requested by HICCs/faculty continue through the approval workflow before becoming live.
